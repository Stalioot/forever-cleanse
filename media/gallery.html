FOREVER CLEANSE
01 · THE GRID

Decurse and dispel,
made easy.

One square per group member. When someone has a debuff you can remove, the square lights up in its colour. Click it and the right spell is cast.

SoloPartyRaidWorks in combat
02 · CLICK TO REMOVE

Two buttons.
Every cure.

Left click casts your first spell, right click the second. The bar on top shows your spell and the colours it removes.

04 · RAID

From party
to raid.

5, 10 or 40 members, five per row, each raid group on its own row. Members out of range fade out, so you know who you can reach.

05 · TWO GRID MODES

Only the afflicted,
or everyone.

Only afflicted: a square appears when there is something to remove. Everyone: every member has a square, with the class colour underneath, lit when needed.
