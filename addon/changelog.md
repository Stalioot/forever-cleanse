### 0.1.0

First beta.

- One square per group member (solo, party, raid), 5 per row in group order. A square lights up when that member has a debuff you can remove: border in the type colour, type symbol, debuff icon and duration. Works in combat.
- Click to remove: left button = first spell, right button = second spell (Mage, Druid, Priest, Paladin, Shaman).
- Options window (gear on the bar, right-click, `/fc`) with a clickable preview and an info page: grid mode, pulsing border, out-of-range fade, enemy buff box for Purge / Dispel Magic, ignore list, size, lock, English / Italiano.
