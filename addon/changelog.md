### 0.2.1

- WoW: Forever's experience auras (Boosted Rest, the BONUS XP icon, and the other XP and rested auras) are always hidden: they are not debuffs to remove.
- Names in other alphabets (Russian, Korean, Chinese players) show with the game's fonts instead of empty boxes.

### 0.2.0

- Sound alert: the game itself plays a sound when a debuff you can remove lands, also in combat. Pick one of four game sounds (Flag, Ding, Bell, Ready) in the options; turn it off with the check box.
- Several debuffs on one member: up to four share the square, the icon is cut in one piece each in its own colour (half and half, then quarters). When one is removed the next one moves in.
- Preview: test debuffs now stack on members (add as many as you like), with a few different icons per type.

### 0.1.0

First beta.

- One square per group member (solo, party, raid), 5 per row in group order. A square lights up when that member has a debuff you can remove: border in the type colour, type symbol, debuff icon and duration. Works in combat.
- Click to remove: left button = first spell, right button = second spell (Mage, Druid, Priest, Paladin, Shaman), picked automatically from your class and known ranks. The bar shows your spell icons and the debuff types they remove.
- Options (gear on the bar, right-click, `/fc`) with a clickable live preview and an info page: grid mode, pulsing border, out-of-range fade, enemy buff box for Purge / Dispel Magic, size, bar opacity, lock, English / Italiano.
- Ignore list: by name or spell ID, or Browse every Curse, Poison, Disease and Magic debuff of WoW: Forever (spell tooltip on hover, all ranks added at once); Reset and Clear all.
